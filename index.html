06 / GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity

goes a long way.

The essentials before we make something together.

How do we get started?

−

Send a few details using the form below. We’ll follow up to understand your goals, confirm availability and put together a project scope.

Can I combine more than one service?

−

Absolutely. A website, visual identity and launch reels can be scoped together so your entire presence feels connected.

What should I have ready?

−

A rough brief, your goals and any existing brand assets are a great start. For video editing, you’ll also need to supply your footage.

How are timelines and revisions agreed?

−

Your proposal sets the schedule, deliverables and review rounds before work starts. Extra requests or ongoing support can be quoted separately.
